First numbered release of Full-Length Player, based on the user-tested milestone 13.

- Two MPV players with shared play/pause, seeking and playback speed.
- Locked alignment, 0.05-second adjustments and automatic audio matching.
- Local files, Patreon/HLS/direct streams and YouTube URL playback.
- Reaction cropping/panning and a centred, top/bottom-anchored source overlay.
- 16:9, 4:3 and 16:10 canvases; fullscreen timeline and cursor auto-hide.
- What Did They Say? commentary replay, independent volumes and track selection.
- Saved sessions, New session and paused loading.
- Six transparent, tightly cropped icons; teal/orange solid is the default.

This is the exact Windows build confirmed by the user, from commit 693b5b6a6f6fc13d0664c734b57d366526e77e88. Launch and playback checks passed in Windows CI and the app was tested on the user's PC via Moonlight.

Download FullLengthPlayer-win-x64.zip, extract it and run FullLengthPlayer.exe with its accompanying files. YouTube may buffer more slowly than the browser; automatic audio matching can need a cleaner section of shared audio.
