Fullscreen refinements following the confirmed milestone 14 beta.

- All brief messages now appear at the top left and last 0.6 seconds, half as long.
- Exit fullscreen has explicit white text on a dark background for clear contrast.
- Hold the left third for 1× or the right third for your favorite speed (default 2×).
  After 450ms, both players temporarily change speed; releasing restores the previous
  speed. Short taps and double-tap seeking remain available.
- Leaving fullscreen, losing focus or moving away cancels a speed hold safely.
- The README gets a solid teal/orange illustrated heading and a new introduction.

The original milestone 14 features have passed user testing. These new refinements
have Windows automated checks but await the user's Moonlight/iPad confirmation.

Download FullLengthPlayer-win-x64.zip, extract it and run FullLengthPlayer.exe with
its accompanying files. Existing settings and sessions remain available.
