# Full Length Player 0.15.0-beta.4 — Eight-position source anchoring

The polished teal/orange UI is retained. Layout now has an eight-position source picker: all four corners and the centre of each edge.

- Orange selection shows where the source is pinned. Hover labels and keyboard-accessible buttons identify each position.
- Source at the top (including corners) anchors the reaction at the bottom; source at the bottom anchors the reaction at the top.
- Left/right midpoints retain the reaction's previous vertical anchor. The reaction never moves sideways automatically.
- Resizing retains the selected anchor and aspect ratio. The fully pinned corner is not a resize handle; use a free corner.
- Saved sessions retain the source position and reaction edge. Existing top/bottom sessions remain compatible.
- Canvas/source Reset and New session restore bottom-centred source and top-anchored reaction.

Extract into a fresh folder. Test all eight positions, corner resizing, fullscreen and session save/restore with your usual 4:3 and 16:10 displays. Beta.3 remains available for comparison. New anchor sessions require beta.4 or later; keep a copy of older sessions if comparing older builds.
