# Full Length Player 0.16.0-beta.8 — Keep locked playback together through buffering

- When either player buffers inside the shared playable range, the alignment lock now pauses both. The healthy stream cannot continue running ahead.
- The app waits for both buffers to recover before correcting any drift, then resumes both together. Recovery is anchored to the stalled player's position to avoid skipping its unseen content.
- If already aligned, recovery avoids an extra seek. If realignment is needed, it uses the existing coordinated seek and preserves your play/pause intention.
- Pause during the wait keeps both paused after refill. Cancel wait cancels automatic resume. A new shared seek replaces the waiting target. The existing two-minute wait deadline still applies.
- Diagnostics identify buffer recovery versus ordinary seeking, and count buffering holds.
- Unlocked independent playback and the preamble/ending behavior outside the videos' shared range are unchanged.

This prevents buffering from pulling a locked pair apart; it does not promise faster YouTube downloads. The reported CDN reaction plus local-source combination remains supported without changing stream quality or adding forced buffering when neither player stalls.

Windows regression coverage uses real HTTP/local media with controlled cache-state signals: either player stalling, refill with drift, manual pause, cancellation, new seeks during a hold, and unlocked playback. Public YouTube stalls still require user testing.

Please lock two YouTube videos, play at 2x and check that a stall now holds both and resumes them aligned. Also try pause during the wait and your usual CDN/local pairing.
